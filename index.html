Flagship · Working MVP · pre-seed

Engram

The company brain for AI agents.
Organizational memory, made executable: the decision graph, skill engine, and agent runtime, built and shipped solo as founder.

Scroll to read the story →

A

Chapter A · The problem

Companies run on decisions, not documents

Most decisions are undocumented, living in senior people's heads and buried Slack threads, and they walk out the door the day someone leaves. Your AI agents inherit the model's intelligence but none of your company's. The #1 blocker on every agent pilot: “it doesn't know how we do things.”

0Curves just crossed: agents shipped · extraction got cheap · search-era KM exposed
#1Blocker stalling AI-agent pilots: missing company context
B

Chapter B · The solution

From messy prose to runnable skills

Engram learns how your company actually makes decisions and compiles that knowledge into skills your agents run safely, with a human in the loop.

  • 01Ingest Slack, tickets, docs, runbooks → typed knowledge.
  • 02Decide Decisions, exceptions & rules → first-class objects.
  • 03Compile The graph compiles into executable, scored skills.
  • 04Act Agents run the skills, autonomous where it's safe.

Runnable MVP today · FastAPI · Next.js · Neo4j / Postgres

C

Chapter C · The moat

Everyone retrieves. We execute

Search shows the policy. Engram compiles the decision into a deterministic, provenance-linked workflow an agent can run, with inputs, steps, and guardrails included. Then a learning loop turns every human override into a sharper brain that compounds per customer, and never transfers to a competitor.

0Live confidence score on the demo's refund skill, pausing for a human under guard
∞A learning loop that compounds with use and never transfers to a competitor
D

Chapter D · The opportunity

Priced by automation depth

Land one function (mid-market support / CX ops), then expand across IT, RevOps, HR, and engineering runbooks. Pricing rises with auto-run depth, so every new function is a built-in upgrade event.
