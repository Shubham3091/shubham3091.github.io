Flagship — Live product

Attribyte

One catalog. Every channel. Always current.
The catalog backbone for D2C brands selling on more than one channel — built, shipped, and reshaped solo as founder-PM.

A

Chapter A — The problem

Catalog drift is a daily tax

D2C brands stopped being single-channel. Prices fall out of sync, attributes disagree, inventory lies — every day, across Shopify, Amazon, Faire, and TikTok Shop. The only alternative: enterprise PIM at $50k–$500k a year and 6-month deploys.

0Median channels per D2C brand — up from 1.2 in 2021
0%YoY growth of multi-channel brands vs 9% Shopify-only
B

Chapter B — The reshape

I killed my own roadmap

v1.2 framed Attribyte as an AI onboarding tool that pushes to Shopify. I ran the frame through twelve falsification tests — it failed all of them. A feature, not a company. So v1.3 reshaped it into the synchronization layer: onboarding is the first 30 minutes of a customer's life; the next two years are the sync.

0Falsification tests run against the original frame
≤0dActivation redefined: second channel connected within 14 days
C

Chapter C — The product

Three layers, one schema

  • L1Ingestion Drop CSV / XLSX / JSON / PDF. AI maps supplier columns to the canonical schema in minutes — with memory, so no column is mapped twice.
  • L2Synchronization Direct-write connectors — Shopify, Amazon SP-API, Faire, TikTok Shop, headless webhooks. Rate-limit-aware, idempotent, bi-directional.
  • L3Governance Continuous drift detection, one-click fixes, audit log, restore-to-last-good. The reason the product opens daily, not quarterly.

Stack: Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · Claude · Vercel

D

Chapter D — The outcome

Pricing now follows value

Pricing moved from SKU count to connected channels × SKUs — every new channel is a natural upgrade event. ACV roughly quadruples; the north star becomes active brands with 2+ connected channels.

$0MReachable ARR at the high end — ~4× the prior frame
